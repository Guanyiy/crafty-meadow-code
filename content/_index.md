## Welcome!

I am a master's student in **Applied Statistics** at the University of Michigan.

My research focuses on developing **interpretable, diagnostic-driven statistical workflows** for real-world data—particularly when empirical data deviate from ideal modeling assumptions.

---

## Research Interests

- **Measurement & Cognitive Aging**  
  HCAP cohorts: United States, Mexico, Nepal  

- **Diagnostics & Robust Modeling**  
  Overlap, measurement validity, zero inflation  

- **High-dimensional Behavioral Data**  
  PCA, sparse PCA, clustering  

- **Causal Inference Under Imperfect Overlap**

- **Semiparametric and Misspecification-Robust Methods**

---

## Current Work

I am currently working with **Dr. Emily Briceño** on cognitive aging across cross-national HCAP cohorts and on measurement adaptation for low-literacy populations.
